The archive

NeoCache87 is a personal archive of machines, games, media, and objects that deserve to be remembered. Each artifact carries a small part of the past into the near future.

Why 87?

On 14 April 1987, I saw Duran Duran for the first time. That date became the origin signal behind the 87: a precise point where music, memory, and the future connected.

The signal continues

The archive will keep growing as more artifacts are recovered, photographed, tagged, and returned to the signal.
